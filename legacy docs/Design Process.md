# **SALTED OLIVE PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT PROCESS**

## **Purpose**

The purpose of product development at Salted Olive is to create dishes, drinks, desserts, and pantry products that strengthen our identity as a Mediterranean-style bistro built around proprietary flavour systems.

Product development should be structured enough to create momentum, but flexible enough to encourage creativity.

The goal is not to launch more dishes.

The goal is to create dishes and pantry products that guests love, that fit the Salted Olive brand, and that support business growth.

---

# **Stage 1: Idea Bank**

## **Objective**

Maintain a continuous collection of ideas from across the business.

Ideas can come from:

* Founder observations  
* Chef ideas  
* Team suggestions  
* Customer feedback  
* Travel experiences  
* Competitor visits  
* Supplier recommendations  
* Industry trends

No evaluation takes place at this stage.

All ideas are welcome.

---

## **Idea Submission Format**

Every idea should include:

### **Idea Name**

### **Bistro Format**

Examples:

* Pot pie  
* Croquette  
* Steak frites  
* Salad  
* Dessert  
* Beverage

### **Mediterranean Foundation**

What makes this Mediterranean?

### **Pantry Product**

Which Salted Olive pantry product does it feature?  
Examples:

* Hot Honey  
* Mint Honey  
* Harissa  
* Salted Olive Dip  
* Chicken Spice Rub  
* Eggplant Rub

### **Why Interesting?**

One sentence only.

# **Stage 2: Monthly Selection**

## **Objective**

Select a small number of concepts for testing.

Frequency:  
Once per month

Participants:

* Founders  
* \[optional\] Head Chef

Duration:  
30–60 minutes

---

Review the idea bank and select:

### **3–5 concepts maximum**

Questions:

* Does this feel like Salted Olive?  
* Does it align with our Product Development Principles?  
* Does it showcase a pantry product?  
* Can ingredients be sourced reasonably?  
* Are we excited to try it?

If yes, move it into R\&D.

No scoring is required at this stage.

---

## **Output**

Monthly R\&D List

Maximum 3–5 concepts.

---

# **Stage 3: Monthly R\&D Day**

## **Objective**

Bring ideas to life as quickly as possible.

Frequency:  
Once per month

Recommended:  
First Tuesday of every month

Duration:  
Half-day to full-day

Participants:

* Founders  
* Head Chef  
* Key kitchen team members

---

## **Rules**

Cook first.  
Judge later.

The objective is not perfection.

The objective is to determine whether the idea has potential.

Prototype dishes should be:

* Fast  
* Rough  
* Honest

Not menu-ready.

Not presentation-perfect.

Just real enough to taste.

---

## **Discussion Topics**

During tasting, discuss:

### **Taste**

Would we genuinely want to eat this again?

### **Salted Olive Fit**

Does it feel like our brand?

### **Pantry Potential**

Does it showcase the sauce, spice blend, or condiment effectively?

### **Commercial Potential**

Can we imagine customers ordering this?

### **Operational Fit**

Can we realistically execute this consistently?

---

## **Scoring**

Score each category:

1–5

Maximum Score:  
25

---

## **Decision Thresholds**

### **20–25**

Proceed to customer testing

### **15–19**

Refine and revisit

### **Below 15**

Archive

---

## **One Additional Rule**

I'd add a veto rule:

**Any dish scoring 2 or below on Operational Fit cannot proceed to customer testing, regardless of total score.**

---

## **Output**

R\&D Scorecard

List of concepts approved for customer testing.

---

# **Stage 4: Salted Olive Test Kitchen**

## **Objective**

Gather feedback from real customers before launch.

Frequency:  
Quarterly

Format:  
Invitation-only dinner

Capacity:  
20–30 guests

---

## **Guest Mix**

Prioritise:

* Loyal customers  
* High-spending customers  
* Friends of the brand  
* Industry contacts  
* Selected creators

---

## **Philosophy**

The event should feel like participation, not research.

Guests should feel invited into the creative process.

This is not a survey session.

This is a Salted Olive experience.

---

## **Feedback Questions**

For each dish:

### **Would you order this?**

### **Would you order this again?**

### **How much would you expect to pay?**

### **Which dish should become permanent?**

### **Which pantry product would you buy?**

---

## **Output**

Customer Feedback Summary

List of dishes approved for real-world testing.

---

# **Stage 5: Limited Release**

## **Objective**

Validate performance in a real operating environment.

Duration:  
2–6 weeks

Examples:

* Monthly Special  
* Seasonal Feature  
* Ramadan Menu  
* Test Kitchen Winner

---

## **Success Measures**

Track:

### **Sales**

### **Customer Feedback**

### **Repeat Orders**

### **Average Spend Impact**

### **Interest in Pantry Products**

---

The question is simple:

Did customers actually choose this dish when paying with their own money?

---

## **Output**

Performance Review

Launch recommendation.

---

# **Stage 6: Decision**

## **Objective**

Determine the future of the concept.

Possible outcomes:

### **Permanent Menu Item**

### **Seasonal Return**

### **Pantry Product Launch**

### **Future Rework**

### **Retirement**

---

## **Decision Criteria**

A product should remain only if it:

* Fits the Salted Olive brand  
* Performs commercially  
* Excites customers  
* Can be executed consistently  
* Strengthens the Salted Olive pantry ecosystem

---

# **Annual Rhythm**

Every Month:

* Idea Collection  
* Monthly Selection  
* R\&D Day

Every Quarter:

* Salted Olive Test Kitchen

As Needed:

* Limited Releases  
* Menu Launches  
* Pantry Product Launches

---

# **Guiding Principle**

The most important part of the process is not the paperwork.

The most important part of the process is protecting time for R\&D.

Ideas only become products when they are cooked.

Therefore, the Monthly R\&D Day is the heart of the Salted Olive product development system.
