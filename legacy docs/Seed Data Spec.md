\# Salted Olive Culinary Intelligence  
\#\# Seed Data Specification

The following data should be automatically seeded into the database during initial setup.

These values are derived from Salted Olive's existing product development framework and should be editable through the admin interface.

\---

\# TABLE: identity\_principles

\#\# Mediterranean First

Category:  
Core Philosophy

Definition:  
Mediterranean culinary principles should be visible in most menu items through ingredients, flavor logic, techniques, or dining rituals.

Notes:  
The goal is not strict authenticity.  
The goal is reinterpretation through the Salted Olive lens.

\---

\#\# Comfort Over Cleverness

Category:  
Core Philosophy

Definition:  
A guest should enjoy a dish before they admire it.

Novelty should support satisfaction, not replace it.

Notes:  
Avoid creating dishes that require explanation before enjoyment.

\---

\#\# Pantry-Led Innovation

Category:  
Core Philosophy

Definition:  
Pantry products are strategic assets that create differentiation and strengthen the Salted Olive ecosystem.

Notes:  
Pantry products should contribute meaningfully to the dining experience rather than function as decorative additions.

\---

\#\# Bistro Format

Category:  
Operating Principle

Definition:  
Food should feel approachable, casual, generous, and suitable for everyday dining.

Notes:  
Avoid fine dining complexity and presentation-driven concepts.

\---

\# TABLE: menu\_roles

\#\# Revenue Driver

Definition:  
Designed primarily to drive sales volume.

Characteristics:  
\- Broad appeal  
\- High familiarity  
\- Repeat order potential

Success Indicator:  
Consistent sales volume.

\---

\#\# Margin Driver

Definition:  
Designed primarily to improve profitability.

Characteristics:  
\- Efficient ingredients  
\- Operational simplicity  
\- Strong margins

Success Indicator:  
Strong contribution margin.

\---

\#\# Brand Driver

Definition:  
Designed primarily to express Salted Olive identity.

Characteristics:  
\- Distinctive  
\- Memorable  
\- Pantry-forward

Success Indicator:  
Customers associate the dish with Salted Olive.

\---

\#\# Pantry Driver

Definition:  
Designed primarily to showcase pantry products.

Characteristics:  
\- Demonstrates pantry versatility  
\- Highlights pantry flavor contributions

Success Indicator:  
Increased pantry awareness and adoption.

\---

\#\# Seasonal Driver

Definition:  
Designed primarily to create novelty and seasonal relevance.

Characteristics:  
\- Time-limited  
\- Exploratory  
\- Conversation-generating

Success Indicator:  
Customer excitement and engagement.

\# TABLE: pantry\_expression\_types

\#\# Hero

Definition:  
The pantry product is central to the dish's identity and flavor.

Example:  
Harissa Chicken

\---

\#\# Supporting

Definition:  
The pantry product contributes meaningfully but is not the primary focus.

Example:  
Olive Relish Steak Sandwich

\---

\#\# Finishing

Definition:  
The pantry product is applied near the end to provide contrast or enhancement.

Example:  
Mint Honey Drizzle

\---

\#\# Hidden

Definition:  
The pantry product contributes to flavor but is not explicitly noticeable.

Example:  
Harissa incorporated into a braising liquid.

\---

\#\# None

Definition:  
No pantry product used.

\# TABLE: default\_experience\_dimensions

These should be created but remain editable.

\#\# Familiarity

Definition:  
How easily a guest can predict the dish before tasting.

Scale:  
1 \= Unexpected  
10 \= Instantly Recognizable

\---

\#\# Discovery

Definition:  
How much newness exists in the experience.

Scale:  
1 \= Predictable  
10 \= Highly Novel

\---

\#\# Comfort

Definition:  
Degree of emotional and sensory reassurance.

Scale:  
1 \= Challenging  
10 \= Comforting

\---

\#\# Freshness

Definition:  
Perception of brightness, lightness, and vitality.

Scale:  
1 \= Heavy  
10 \= Fresh

NOTE:  
This dimension is currently considered provisional and should be validated during workshops.

\---

\#\# Shareability

Definition:  
Extent to which a dish encourages social dining.

Scale:  
1 \= Individual  
10 \= Highly Shareable

NOTE:  
This dimension is currently considered provisional and should be validated during workshops.
